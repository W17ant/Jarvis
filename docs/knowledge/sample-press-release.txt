Aston Martin reveals Vulcan AMR Pro at Goodwood

The track-only Vulcan AMR Pro pushes 820 horsepower through a naturally aspirated 7.0-litre V12. Lap times around the Top Gear test track are claimed to undercut the LaFerrari by 1.4 seconds.

Built in a run of 24, each car is hand-finished at the Gaydon facility. Pricing is £2.3 million plus local taxes.

Owners attend two days of trackside coaching at Silverstone as part of the package.
